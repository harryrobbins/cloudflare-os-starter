Your data has a meaning before it has an app.

Records is a standards-based app datastore. Start with an open model, extend it, or define your own. Apps share the model, permissions and history.

The service is deployed. Postgres, PostgREST, a gateway and an authorised change relay are implemented. The website and API run at records.surprisingly.ltd on a homeserver through Cloudflare Tunnel. Business-data qualification and full cloudflare-os integration remain delivery gates.

What exists today?

Executable work and messaging modules, a separately publishable inventory example, a TypeScript client, scoped credentials, command history and sync primitives. Rich documents, arbitrary database federation and vendor-compatible SDKs are separate future work.
