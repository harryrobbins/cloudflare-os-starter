Channel economics

One 500-seat customer in its first year, sold four ways. Direct earns the most per deal and costs the most founder time. The partner route gives away a quarter of the licence and the implementation, and needs nothing from us but the software, which is how a ten-person company reaches 25,000 seats.

Founder sales time is costed at £1,300 a day. Sales-cycle lengths are our experience, grade C.
