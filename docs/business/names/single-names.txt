What each name was tested against
Companies House. No active UK company registered under the bare word, once the disregarded words (limited, group, uk, holdings…) are stripped from both ends — the same-as rule that governs whether registration is refused. No active UK company carrying the word under an IT SIC code. Fewer than 25 active companies containing the word at all.

Trademarks. No live UK mark for the exact word in Nice classes 9, 35, 38 and 42 — software, business services, telecoms and IT consultancy. Class 42 is the binding one.

Domains. Checked by RDAP against each registry directly, with .co.uk confirmed only where RDAP and a live DNS query agree — Nominet's whois rate-limits and reports false negatives when queried in a loop.

Collisions worldwide. Searched for existing use by consultancies, agencies and AI or software products. Judged on the forgiving standard you set: a US games company is not a conflict for a UK consulting and AI agency, but an AI product is.
