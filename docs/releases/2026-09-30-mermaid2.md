## Recovery

The previous Workshop version above is the recovery baseline. Rolling Workshop
back restores its earlier bindings and UI configuration; it does not remove the
installed blueprint metadata or any new gadget drafts. MermaiD2 connections
require the new renderer binding, so they may stop working while Workshop is on
the old version. Keep the renderer and all storage; do not delete or recreate
resources as an automatic rollback step. Inventory actual versions and data
before coordinating a rollback or roll-forward.
